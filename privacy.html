Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

AF Video Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used by its owner to upload videos to YouTube channels they own. This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

Information the app accesses

When a channel owner signs in with Google and clicks "Allow," the app receives two permissions for that YouTube channel:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, schedule and privacy settings.
  • youtube: to add the uploaded videos to the channel's own playlists, and to read the channel's name and ID so the app can confirm the right channel is connected.

The app uses these permissions only for those tasks. It does not read, change or delete other videos, comments, subscribers or account details.

How information is stored

The sign-in access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. The app does not run a server, does not keep a database of users, and does not collect analytics, cookies or personal information.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any information with anyone. Video files and details are sent only to YouTube, through the YouTube API.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. Information sent to YouTube is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

AF Video Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When you do, the saved access token stops working, and you can delete it from the owner's computer.

Contact

Questions about this policy: afyoutubeuploader@gmail.com
