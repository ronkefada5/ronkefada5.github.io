AF Video Uploader

AF Video Uploader is a private tool used by its owner to upload videos they create to YouTube channels they own and manage.

It uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, schedule and privacy settings, and add them to the channel's own playlists. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect, sell or share anyone's personal data.

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
